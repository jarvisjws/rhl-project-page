How to interpret these results

Each identity is counted once if its original task goal was reached. Versions, retry counts and horizons vary. RoboTwin's six historical exclusions and RoboDojo's fixed batch definition remain part of the interpretation. These examples do not measure comparative efficiency, autonomous authorship across all historical work, or generalization to unseen tasks. Full task-goal success is distinct from complete physical qualification. Twin Pick49 and Handover3028 retain author-reviewed task-goal acceptance; their original framework checks remain unchanged. Cumulative inventory success is not automatically a same-run native evaluator PASS.

The six attempted RoboTwin exclusions are historical human scope decisions: hammer seeds 3006, 3009 and 3011 (designated initial grasp classified unreachable); handover 3015 (occluded designated block); mug 5032 (human judgment, specific physical rationale undocumented); and pot 7147 (pot flown out). They are not proofs of impossibility under every strategy. Seven other pre-attempt exclusions are outside the 121 and are not subtracted again. Ordinary unsuccessful cases remain in the denominator.

03 / KNOWLEDGE REVISION & REUSE

Learn a procedure.
Instantiate it from a new state.

The xylophone example connects an original failure, a revised execution, and later consumers of the retained geometric rule. It also preserves a later failure.

Original solutionA fixed world orientation→Retained procedureDerive the operation from
the held object, target and arm base

The failed procedure imposed a fixed mallet yaw. The revision computes a radial yaw and compensating translation from state, preserving the required beat points and eight-key sequence. Later runs call the same retained procedure with their own held-object relation; they do not replay one saved angle.

Observed reuse, with limits.

The retained rule was consumed in 24 previously failed layouts: 19 succeeded and 5 did not, under expanded horizons. These records establish actual consumption, not an isolated causal benefit or transfer to unseen tasks.
